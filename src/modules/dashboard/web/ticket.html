Scenarios

Each button opens the same modal on its own mock. Sizing works like Rabby — you move margin and leverage, notional follows. You do not type a stop: the nanny places it, so the “Stop loss” row shows what it will do rather than asking for input.

What to poke
  • The margin slider at 3× — below $3.34 notional falls under the exchange minimum of $10, the number turns red and the same notice Rabby shows appears.
  • The leverage slider — size in coins recomputes immediately.
  • Order type toggles inline (Market / Limit post-only); a limit order gets a price field, and a price that would cross the market is caught before sending.
  • Price in an open modal refreshes itself every 5 seconds.
  • Esc or a backdrop click closes it; on a narrow window the panel slides up as a sheet.
