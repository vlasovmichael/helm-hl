How to use this page
  1. Pick a coin and timeframe in the chart below — the zones redraw for it.
  2. Choose the side you are considering: Long or Short.
  3. Type the price you would enter at, or press Use market.
  4. Read the verdict. Either the ratio clears the floor, or there is no trade.
  5. At market price the ratio is usually poor. Ready entries below lists the prices at the zones where it is not — press Use to load one.

The page never says where price will go. It answers one question: given this entry, is there a level to put the stop behind and a level to aim at, and does what is left over pay for the risk and the fees.
