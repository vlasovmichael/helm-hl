WALL = a resting order much larger than its neighbours. A real level where money sits.

How to read it
  • Wall above = ceiling. Do not long into it — that is where sellers sit.
  • Wall below = floor. A long off it with the stop under the wall keeps risk tight.
  • Bids >> asks = buyers pressing, backdrop favours upside. And vice versa.
  • Wall vanished and price walked through = the level broke.
  • On the exit: if there is a wall ahead, take profit before it.
