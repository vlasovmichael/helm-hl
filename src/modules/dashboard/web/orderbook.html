How to read it (entry and exit)

• Wall above = ceiling. Do not long into it — that is where sellers sit (like HYPE 64.5).
• Wall below = floor. A long off it with the stop under the wall keeps risk tight.
• Bids >> asks = buyers pressing, backdrop favours upside. And vice versa.
• Wall vanished and price walked through = the level broke, the move is tradable.
• On the exit: if there is a wall ahead, take profit before it instead of hoping it breaks.
