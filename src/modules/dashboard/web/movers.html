# Coin Setup Enter Price 2m% 5m% 15m% Acc OI 5m Trend
Chasing · is the move already gone

Answers exactly one question per coin: is this side an entry into a move that already happened, or against it. From 650 manual trades (25.05–02.09.2026): entering with the 15m trend returned −0.18 per trade against −0.05 entering against it; an entry while the hour is down more than 5% returned −0.49 (the only slice surviving a Bonferroni correction over ~17 slices). Longs −38.77, shorts +22.89. It picks no coin and predicts nothing — a flag means the journal paid dearly for that side, and silence is not a green light. The hypothesis is post-hoc, registered as entry-into-continuation, and is judged once at 60 fresh trades — not before.
